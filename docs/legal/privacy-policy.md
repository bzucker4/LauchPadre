This policy explains what personal data LaunchPadre ("we", "us") handles when you use [PRODUCT_URL] and why. We keep it short because we collect little.

## 1. Data we collect

| Data | Why | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Email address | Create and secure your account; verification and password-reset emails; account and billing notices | You |
| Password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash) | Sign you in | You |
| Session cookie `lp_customer` (random token; only its hash is stored; expires after 8 hours) | Keep you signed in | Generated at sign-in |
| Verification and reset tokens (stored hashed; expire in 24 hours / 1 hour) | Email verification and password reset | Generated by us |
| Repository names, task titles and statuses, checkpoint names | Provide the workspace | You |
| Git repositories you ask us to host/connect, including their code and commit history | Provide checkpoints and restore, and Zenodo archiving | You / the operator who provisions them |
| Plan and subscription status, Stripe customer and subscription identifiers | Provide paid features | Stripe |
| Technical request data (IP address, user agent, timestamps, request path, error and security logs) | Operate and secure the Service, rate-limit sign-in attempts, block abusive or AI-training crawlers | Your browser |

We do **not** collect payment-card numbers (Stripe does), and we do not use advertising or analytics cookies or trackers. The only cookie we set is the strictly necessary session cookie above.

## 2. How we use it and our legal bases
- **To provide the Service** (account, workspace, billing, emails you trigger): performance of our contract with you.
- **To secure and operate the Service** (rate limiting, abuse prevention, logs, backups): our legitimate interests in running a safe service.
- **To meet legal obligations** (tax, accounting, responding to lawful requests).
- **With your consent** where we ask for it (for example, publishing to Zenodo is something you explicitly confirm).

We do not sell your personal data, do not use it for advertising, and do not use your repositories or tasks to train AI models.

## 3. Who we share data with
We use service providers who process data on our behalf:
- **Stripe**: payments, subscriptions and the billing portal. Stripe is an independent controller for payment and fraud-prevention data under its own privacy policy.
- **Resend**: sends our verification and password-reset emails (receives your email address and the message content).
- **Hosting/infrastructure provider [HOSTING_PROVIDER]**: runs the servers, database and backups.
- **Zenodo (CERN)**: only if you use Academic archiving. See section 4.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect rights, safety and the security of the Service. If the business is sold or merged, data may transfer to the successor under this policy.

## 4. Publishing to Zenodo
If you archive a repository, we send the committed contents of your repository's HEAD (never `.git` or uncommitted files) to Zenodo on your instruction. After you confirm publication, **it becomes public with a permanent DOI and generally cannot be removed**, including by us. Zenodo is a separate controller; its policies govern what it does with the data. Review contents for personal data and secrets before confirming.

## 5. International transfers
Our providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the provider's certification under an approved framework.

## 7. Your rights
Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR/UK GDPR or CCPA/CPRA) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict use of your personal data, to object to processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data-protection authority. Email [CONTACT_EMAIL] from your account email. We will verify the request and respond within 30 days. You can also do this yourself in the app under **Your Data**: download a JSON export of your account, repositories, tasks and archive records, or permanently delete your account (which also cancels your subscription immediately and deletes your hosted Git data and any unpublished Zenodo drafts). The export does not include repository source code or Git checkpoints; email us if you want a Git bundle of repositories we host for you.

We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law, and we do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

## 8. Security
Passwords are salted and hashed with scrypt; sessions use HttpOnly, SameSite cookies over HTTPS with cross-site request protections; tokens are stored hashed; customers' data is isolated per account; and sign-in is rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

## 9. Children
The Service is not directed to anyone under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Do Not Track and crawlers
We do not track you across sites, so there is nothing for Do Not Track to disable. The Service serves `noindex` headers and blocks known AI-training crawlers.
